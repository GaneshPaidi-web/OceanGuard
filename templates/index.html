How AquaGuard AI Works

Step 01 📡

Scene Detection

Automatically identifies surface CCTV, underwater RGB, or sonar grayscale footage and applies tailored pre-processing.

Step 02 🎯

D-FINE Person Detection

Tiled D-FINE-S ONNX model scans the full frame with 25% overlap tiles for maximum detection recall.

Step 03 🦴

Pose Estimation

MediaPipe extracts 33 body landmarks per person — shoulder tilt, head position, arm asymmetry, and visibility score.

Step 04 🧠

Risk Classification

Seven drowning indicators are fused — body angle, stationary duration, thrashing asymmetry, and submersion — to output NORMAL / DISTRESS / DROWNING.

Step 05 🔔

Instant Alerts

Color-coded bounding boxes, alert banner overlays, audio beeps, and downloadable alert CSV export.

● NORMAL — Safe swimmer ● DISTRESS — Needs attention ● DROWNING — Emergency! ● UNKNOWN — Building track
